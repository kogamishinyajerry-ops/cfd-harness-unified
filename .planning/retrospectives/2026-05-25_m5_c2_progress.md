## 关键发现 · bug #5 (streamline filename mismatch) — RESOLVED (`b6c8209`)

Initially mis-diagnosed as a cross-fork (ESI-2312-vs-OF10) tracking issue. The
**actual** root cause: `postProcess -func streamlines` resolves the config at
`system/streamlines`, but `_build_streamlines_dict` wrote `system/streamlinesDict`,
so OpenFOAM silently fell back to the **built-in streamlines preset** (a
default single-seed config) → "seeded 1 particles" regardless of our dict.
Renaming the written file to `system/streamlines` makes our 60-seed mesh-bbox
grid take effect. The ESI 2312 image tracks particles through the OF10-solved
mesh fine (same as foamToVTK) — **no OF10-container migration needed; the
cross-fork theory was wrong.** Diagnosis method: copied the dict to
`system/streamlines` and re-ran — immediately got "seeded 54 particles".

**Verified live** (backward_step `imported_2026-05-25T12-35-40Z`): streamLine
"seeded 54 particles · Tracks:54 · Total samples:43336" (6 of 60 seeds
correctly dropped inside the solid step — grid coverage works); GET
/post/streamlines.vtp → 1.85 MB / 43336 points / 54 lines / real U field;
V4 Post visual: 54 |U|-colored tracks render through the channel, velocity
legend driven by the real VTP range **0→0.86 m/s** (was the [0,40] fallback).

## 关键发现 · no-slip wall surfaces are U≈0 (physics, not a bug)

The surface overlay colors a patch by `|U|`. For internal-flow cases (LDC,
backward_step) the wall patches are **no-slip → U≈0 everywhere → uniform
color + degenerate scalar range**, so the velocity legend stays at its
fallback rather than being "driven by the real VTP range." The moving `lid`
patch is uniform at lid-velocity (also degenerate). The genuinely varying
velocity field lives in the **volume** (shown by the M4 C3 matplotlib report
figure — which works) and along **streamlines** (blocked by bug #5).
Coloring the surface by **pressure** `p` (which varies on walls) would make
the surface overlay informative for all cases, but that changes the charter's
"velocity legend" intent → a design decision for the user.
