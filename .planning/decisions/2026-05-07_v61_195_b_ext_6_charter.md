## Charter

**Top-line goal:** make Variant A of the Step 6 isolation rehearsal
(real backward_step brief, L/h reattachment) reach `submit_verdict`
instead of `submit_drop`. The verdict's pass/fail outcome is
secondary — what matters is that the persona has access to enough
data to compute and submit a numeric observed_value.

A real-physics verdict pass (where observed L/h falls within
6.0±10% of Kim 1980 reference) is a stretch goal that depends on
the upstream BC path being correct. If the prestage uses LDC
defaults on backward_step geometry (current Variant A path), the
observed L/h will be physically wrong but well-defined; the persona
should submit_verdict with a low-confidence rationale, the verdict
will register `passed=false`, and the chain will be fully exercised.
