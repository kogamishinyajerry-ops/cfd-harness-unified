# DEC-V61-191 · B-ext-5 charter

## Status

**Accepted 2026-05-07** — opens B-ext-5 with a deliberate scope pivot
away from the B-ext-2/3/4 strategy. Five R-iterations across three
arcs landed real workbench fixes (F9 / F10 / F11 / F12) without ever
producing a charter verdict pass. This charter abandons the
"3-cell × 3-persona × monolithic verdict" framing and focuses on
unlocking the one promising signal R8 surfaced (backward_step
self-driven /solve POST 200 + clean measurement.yaml).

## Context (load-bearing)

- **R8 high-water mark:** backward_step / novice produced
  `reports/imported_..1e6fcecf/runs/2026-05-07T10-37-10Z/measurement.yaml`
  with `success=true`, p=6.5e-7, continuity=9.9e-12. **First and only**
  persona-driven /solve POST 200 across the full B-ext arc.
- **R9 regression:** same cell crashed at step 20 on a DeepSeek read
  timeout (F14, external API). The R8 signal is valid but **not
  stable** under repeated runs.
- **naca0012:** immune to anti-mesh-cycle prompts across R5/R6/R7/R8/R9
  (5 iterations). Persona consistently consumes all max_steps on
  geometric/mesh discovery and never reaches /solve. Fixing this
  belongs to "persona prompt engineering" + "case-cell selection",
  not to workbench changes.
- **F13 surfaced in R9:** /solve 502 Bad Gateway 11× across naca0012 +
  pipe_expansion. Workbench `/api/health` healthy throughout; ephemeral
  OpenFOAM container `compassionate_neumann` lingered 42 min. Likely
  solver-spawn race or container resource pressure under sustained
  automation. Not deterministic (R7 curl + R8 backward_step both got
  200).

## Charter

**Top-line goal:** produce **1/1 verdict pass on backward_step / novice**
on a stable, reproducible run, with V130 advisory-only contract intact.

This replaces the B-ext-2/3/4 charter (verdict pass ≥ 1/3 across 3
cells). Rationale: R8 already proved the workbench chain works for
backward_step end-to-end; the remaining gap is persona budget +
verdict-formation prompt + external-API reliability — all of which
are tractable in a 1-cell focus.
