The committed failed run is GOOD demo material — it proves the bridge handles real-world imperfection, not just success cases. **Lesson**: when the only real artifact is a failure, lean into it. Curated narratives describe the ideal; bridge mode describes reality; the gap between them is exactly what V6.C diff panel exists to surface.

Surface placeholders so reviewers can audit them.
