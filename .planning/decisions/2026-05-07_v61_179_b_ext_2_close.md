## Strategic takeaway

B-ext-2 is the third successive iteration where each round delivers
real value (a fix, a prompt extension, a finding) but the headline
metric (verdict pass) doesn't move. R3→R4→R4.5 fixed F1-F7 and
moved Step-5 reach 0→2/3. R5+R6 fixed F9 and identified F10 but
verdict pass stayed at 0.

**The verdict-pass charter target was correct in spirit but
underestimated workbench-side surface depth.** Each R-iteration
discovers the next bug in the integrate-then-test direction. F10 is
the seventh substantive fix needed to land verdict — at the current
rate, B-ext-3 + B-ext-4 may be needed before a clean E2E verdict
pass on a single cell.

The honest path forward: **stop iterating R-rounds on persona
prompts alone**. Open B-ext-3 to fix F10 in the workbench codebase,
then a single R7 should suffice to confirm verdict pass.
