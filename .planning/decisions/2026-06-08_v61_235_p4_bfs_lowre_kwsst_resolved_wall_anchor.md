### The dual-method V&V finding (why wall-shear, not a fixed-height U_x proxy)

A y=0.02 U_x probe reads 5.65 on the high-Re mesh (first cell) but is grid-height
biased on the resolved mesh (y=0.02 is ~15–20 cells up). Re-running the high-Re case
through the identical wall-shear pipeline gives Xr/H=5.647 — matching this project's
documented high-Re value — confirming the extraction is sound and 5.881 is a genuine
physics improvement, not an artifact. The floorProfile-U_x sampler was REMOVED from
the adapter (the wall-shear method is the single anchor).
