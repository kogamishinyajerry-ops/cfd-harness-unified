### Substrate fragility uncovered
- **Real WCAG violation in SolverInflightTicker** (V77 substrate): `<div className="bg-black/50" overflow:auto>` lacked `tabIndex` and `role`. axe-core flagged "scrollable-region-focusable" serious-impact. V78.6 patched with `tabIndex={0} role="log" aria-live="polite" aria-label`. **Lesson**: scrollable containers are an a11y gotcha; future arcs adding any `overflow: auto/scroll` should bake in keyboard access from the first commit.
- **V78.1 backend SSE made V77 baselines drift**: with backend now returning 200 (was 404 in V77 era), `sse-stream-status` testid shows "open"/"connecting" instead of "offline". Baselines 70/72/73 needed re-snap. **Lesson**: the V77 close was hiding a real coupling — frontend tests captured under offline fallback would visibly change once backend went live.
- **Pillar 13/16 rebalancing (25→20 each) is a real scoring reduction**: the OLD V77.5 (4 subscores at 25 each) scoring would have given 100/100 even if backend_sse_e2e didn't pass. V78's 5 subscores at 20 each means failing ANY ONE drops the pillar to 80. Bar genuinely raised.
