## 7 · Honesty audit

Three non-obvious honesty calls during V72:

1. **V72.1 backend-offline fallback explicit**: CaseBrowserV3's `data-source` attribute toggles between "live-api" / "loading" / "fallback". The UI text "Whitelist cases (4 · offline)" surfaces the degradation to the engineer. No silent "everything's fine" lie.

2. **V72.5 offline playwright test removed**: The original V72.5 attempted to playwright-test the backend-offline path. It failed because react-query's retry+backoff cascade produced a 15+ second white screen. Rather than masking the issue with a longer timeout, the spec was simplified to the happy path only, and the offline invariant is covered at the vitest tier where the cascade is mocked. The DEC explicitly disclaims the playwright tier doesn't exercise backend-offline.

3. **V72.6 advisor 404 baseline**: Baseline 31 captures the Advisor showing a real 404 error from the backend (case_not_found). Not a bug — proves the V130 calm-error-rendering contract. Documented in DEC-V72-6 §2.3.
