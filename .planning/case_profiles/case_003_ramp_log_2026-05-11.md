## Substrate-listening findings (4 new, 1 V-series reclassification)

### F-NEW-1 · A1 mechanical extraction validated on second non-APU industrial case
A1's `Import.insert()` pattern preserved Codex-injected body labels through
a FreeCAD subprocess bridge. Confirms A1 is reusable beyond the APU bay
source. **Status**: A1 advisor → field-validated, second case.

### F-NEW-2 · FreeCAD subprocess bridge has positional-arg friction
`freecadcmd` auto-opens any positional argument as "file to open", which
clashes with passing script paths/IO paths via argv. Workaround: sidecar
args file (`_probe_args.txt`) at a known path. Works but is fragile. If
this bridge pattern persists across future sessions, consider a proper
subprocess wrapper service in `geometry_ingest/` (next-spike candidate).

### F-NEW-3 · P0 heuristic conflates airframe-class bodies with CFD domain bodies
When STEP contains both the airframe AND its CFD domain boundaries
(inlet/outlet/farfield), the bbox max extent reflects the **domain**, not
the geometry of interest. Result: P0 flags UNKNOWN even when airframe
bodies themselves are at sensible mm-scale.

Per-body extents in case_003:
- `airframe_reference`: 182.88 m × 91.44 m × 182.88 m (large but not catastrophic)
- `root_mount_pad`: 18.29 m × 1.91 m × 3.05 m
- `thin_access_plate`: 27.43 m × 3.43 m × **0.0008 m** (defect; matches profile D8 = 0.80 mm)
- `inlet`/`outlet`: 0.152 m × 1600 m × 1541 m (CFD domain walls)
- `farfield_*`: 2438 m × 1600 m × 0.152 m (CFD domain top/bottom/outer)
- `symmetry_plane`: 2438 m × 0.152 m × 1541 m

**Spike candidate**: extend P0 to accept a `body_class_filter` callback or
take a list of "airframe bodies vs domain bodies". Or downstream caller
runs P0 only on airframe-class bodies. **Estimate**: ≤40 LOC + test.

### F-NEW-4 · V20 reclassification — case_003 v1 finding likely misdiagnosis
case_003 v1 reported "loaded as 91 m, ~25.4× over expected" suggesting
inch-treated-as-mm. Session 1 evidence contradicts:

- STEP header explicitly declares `SI_UNIT(.MILLI.,.METRE.)` — MM intentional
- Airframe x_length = 91.44 m → bbox is **exactly 3600 inches** (clean number, not artifact)
- Real CRM-HLS half-span ≈ 29.4 m; loaded 91.44 m / 29.4 m ≈ **3.11×** (not 25.4×)
- 25.4× would imply factor of 1 inch = 25.4 mm; observed factor is ~3×

**Hypothesis**: Codex's `build_cad.py` script scaled the airframe by ~3× to
fit a larger CFD domain (or for a different demo purpose). The "V20 unit
issue" is actually a **deliberate scaling artifact**, not a unit-conversion
bug.

**V-series action**: open V20 amendment in `industrial_case_solver_findings.md`:
status = "misdiagnosis confirmed by session 1 probe; actual issue is intentional
3× scale in Codex CAD generator, not unit chain bug".

### F-NEW-5 · Workbench route layer does NOT yet support STEP import
Confirmed: `ui/backend/routes/import_geometry.py` accepts STL only (per M5.0
charter). For case_003 to fully ride the workbench shell, we need either
(a) STEP→STL conversion as a pre-route step, or (b) extend the import
route to accept STEP. Per V198 §S3 substrate philosophy, recommend (a) —
add a `freecad_step_to_stl` helper in `geometry_ingest/` that wraps
freecadcmd to emit per-body STL, then standard STL import path handles
the rest.
