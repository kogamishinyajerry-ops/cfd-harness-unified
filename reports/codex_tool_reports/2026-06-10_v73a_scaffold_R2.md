## Codex summary (verbatim)

> The duplicate-time selection fix is sound, but the new asymmetric span path
> is not. It both accepts genuinely under-sized surfaces and leaves accepted
> short-span cases in a biased x/c frame, so the transonic gate can now
> mis-score or incorrectly admit/reject replay bundles.

## Findings (verbatim)

- [P1] Reject under-sized surfaces instead of treating them as face centres —
  src/transonic_airfoil_extractor.py:536-543
  This new band admits any sampled profile whose span is between `0.90c` and
  `0.98c`, but from `p_aerofoil.raw` alone there is no way to distinguish a
  legitimate face-centre write from a genuinely clipped or mis-scaled
  surface. That means a replay bundle with a real 5-10% chord mismatch will
  now bypass the only geometry/chord guard and be judged as if it were the
  target airfoil, which breaks the extractor's previous fail-closed contract.

- [P2] Renormalize accepted short spans before reporting x/c —
  src/transonic_airfoil_extractor.py:545-547
  Once a short span is accepted, `upper_cp` and `lower_cp` are still built as
  `(x - x_le) / chord`. On a valid coarse mesh whose first face centre sits a
  few percent behind the true leading edge, that shifts every abscissa left
  and under-reports `shock_xc` by the missing LE segment.
  `transonic_airfoil_gate` consumes `shock_xc` as an enforced QoI, so a
  physically correct replay can now fail validation purely because of surface
  discretization.

## Adoption (joint resolution of both findings + retains R1-P2's intent)

The asymmetric band is REMOVED. Replacement: **vertex-recovered chord** —
the extreme face centre sits at most HALF its own end-face length inboard of
the true LE/TE vertex, so `le_true = x_first − gap_le/2`, `te_true = x_last +
gap_te/2`, `chord_est = te_true − le_true`, with end gaps measured to the
first STRICTLY different x (a blunt TE can put two face centres at one x).
Then:
- tight symmetric 2% check restored on `chord_est` vs declared chord
  (P1: a clipped/mis-scaled surface is missing far more than its own
  end-face half-lengths — it cannot hide inside the compensation);
- `x/c = (x − le_true)/chord_est` (P2: no systematic left-shift; also keeps
  R0-P2's origin independence).

Regressions: face-centre midpoint fixture accepted + shock_xc unbiased vs the
vertex fixture; uniform 5% shrink now REJECTED as ambiguous-clipped; 0.85x /
1.05x / 1.5x rejected; duplicate-x blunt-TE plateau handled.
